### 3.8 FR-only body confirmation

**The body is FR-only for ALL 12 keys.** `getResolutionsForType(resolutionType)` at `lib/pdf/generatePdfDocument.ts:62-89` takes a single `resolutionType` argument and does NOT accept a `language` parameter. The map literal contains only FR `title` and `body` strings (lines 64-86). When the caller passes `language: 'en'`, the shell chrome (resolved-clause, sig-labels, subtitle, footer) flips to EN via `LABELS[data.language]`, but the resolution body items themselves stay FR. Tier 3 #75 tracks the broader plumbing gap (also: `bulk-generate` route omits language and `generate-item` defaults to FR).
